Fideo

Description

Fideo is the best soup that has ever existed, rivaled only by Chic-Fil-A's chicken noodle soup. Nah but this is the soup of my childhood and its the only soup I can consistantly make that tastes good.

Ingredients

  • Pasta in your favorite shape. Mine is what my mom called melon seed
  • Two medium sized roma tomatoes or one small and big tomatoe
  • If you like onion, add 3/4 of a medium to large onion. add half or less if you don't really like onion
  • Three cubes of chicken flavor seasoning
  • A lot of water. This really depends how soup you want your soup
  • Oh and salt if you a little freak like that

Steps

  1. Throw in the tomatoes, onion, and cubes in a blender and blend until its a consistant paste
  2. Pour some oil on in pan and throw in your pasta. Stir until light brown
  3. Pour your paste into the pasta when brown and add water to your ideal consistancy
  4. Bring to boil while stirring and turn down to a simmer once boiling
  5. Enjoy some soup young shotty
