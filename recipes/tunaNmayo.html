Tuna N Mayo

Description

Tuna and Mayo is very simple meal that I like to make when I don't have the energy to make anything else. It consist of two simple ingredients but can be made fancier if you have other stuff.

Ingredients

  • Can of Tuna
  • Mayo
  • A Bowl to put it in

Steps

  1. Open can of tuna
  2. Put can of tuna in bowl
  3. Put Mayo in the bowl
  4. Mix and enjoy
